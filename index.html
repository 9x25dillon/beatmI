01Scale lock

Every row on the melody grid is a note inside your key. There is no cell to click that sounds wrong, so you can move fast without second-guessing the pitch.

02Rhythm lock

Point the lock at your kick or hats and the melody can only place notes where those hit. Shared rhythmic DNA is the reason a beat feels glued together.

03Motif engine

Write one good bar. The operators — transpose, invert, retrograde, displace, thin — generate a variation that stays recognisably the same idea across four bars.

04Mud check

A low melody note landing on the same step as the kick gets a red outline. It is the most common reason a mix sounds cluttered at the bottom.
